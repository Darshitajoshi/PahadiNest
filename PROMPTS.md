# Week 7 - AI Integration
## Project: PahadiNest AI Travel Assistant

### Prompt Variation 1

**Prompt:**
Plan a 3-day trip to Chopta.

**Example Output:**
The AI generated a complete 3-day itinerary including arrival, sightseeing, trekking to Tungnath and Chandrashila, accommodation suggestions, local food, and travel tips.

---

### Prompt Variation 2

**Prompt:**
Suggest the best homestays in Mussoorie for a family.

**Example Output:**
The AI recommended family-friendly homestays, nearby attractions, local cuisine, estimated budget, and the best season to visit.

---

### Prompt Variation 3

**Prompt:**
Give me a packing list for the Kedarkantha Trek.

**Example Output:**
The AI provided a categorized packing checklist including clothing, trekking gear, medicines, documents, food, and safety essentials.

---

# Best Prompt

The first prompt, **"Plan a 3-day trip to Chopta"**, produced the most comprehensive response. It generated a structured itinerary with daily activities, travel recommendations, accommodation suggestions, and practical tips. The output was detailed, easy to understand, and highly relevant for travelers planning a trip to Uttarakhand.
